kubeglance is starting.

Open it:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "kubeglance.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  → http://127.0.0.1:8080

From your phone, put it behind Tailscale, Cloudflare Tunnel or an Ingress with auth;
kubeglance itself has no authentication. Add it to your home screen: it is a PWA.
